# Official Cases

This folder keeps a lightweight index of official CUMCM benchmark sources. It
does not store official papers, images, or copied article content.

Use it to check whether the skill knows where to look for:

- contest problem reviews;
- excellent paper showcases;
- expert guidance on good modeling papers.

The eval only validates source coverage and metadata. Actual modeling quality
still requires reading the relevant official material and applying
`references/first-prize-rubric.md`.
